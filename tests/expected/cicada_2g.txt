== Cellular modem (remote attack surface) ==
   [MEDIUM] Cellular modem U4 (SIM800C (ordinary version)) puts the device on the mobile network: reachable from anywhere, not just within radio range
            -> Use a private APN or carrier firewall so the device has no open ports on the internet, use TLS with certificate checks, ignore SMS commands, and keep the modem firmware updated.
   [LOW   ] U4 (SIM800C (ordinary version)) is 2G-only: 2G doesn't authenticate the network, so a fake base station can intercept or inject traffic
            -> Use end-to-end encryption (TLS) for everything. For new designs pick an LTE-M/NB-IoT modem; 2G networks are also being switched off.
   Cybersecurity view:
      Attack    : Reach the device from anywhere through the mobile network - no physical access or
                  radio range needed: probe services on its IP address, send SMS commands, or (on
                  2G) run a fake base station to intercept and inject traffic.
      Attacker  : Cellular - from anywhere over the mobile network (internet, SMS), no physical
                  access needed
      Impact    : Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability
      STRIDE    : Spoofing, Tampering, Information disclosure, Denial of service, Elevation of
                  privilege
      CWE       : CWE-306 (missing authentication for critical function), CWE-319 (cleartext
                  transmission), CWE-923 (communication channel to an unverified endpoint)
